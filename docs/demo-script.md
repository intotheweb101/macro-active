# MacroActive Website Demo Script

## Short walkthrough

Start on the `Overview` page:

“This is a small Laravel and DDEV demo app I put together to show how I’d work in a practical product environment. It covers local setup, product-facing UI, reviewable branches, and release workflow.”

Move to `Creator ops`:

“This page shows the sort of operational visibility a SaaS team needs. You can see onboarding state, health signals, and where support or retention risks start to appear. It is a good example of turning product and ops requirements into something clear and usable.”

Open `Launch assistant`:

“This is the AI example. It is intentionally on-brand and tied to creator launches. The point is not to replace developers or the team, but to use AI where it adds value — in this case helping turn a creator brief into a structured launch plan.”

Finish on `Engineering`:

“From here I can show how the work is broken into focused branches, how the pull requests are reviewable, and how CI/CD supports safe delivery. The combined branch then shows how all of those changes come together into one polished demo environment.”

## Closing line

“The main thing I wanted to show here is that I think about AI, product, and engineering as one delivery system. AI helps with speed and structure, but the team still owns quality, architecture, and what ships.”
